# Sked Portal — Scheduling Policies (source of truth)

Source: "Clinical Hours Policy & Student Acknowledgment" — The Preceptor Site, a division of TMM Medical Group.
Use these rules when writing the student "How To Use the Scheduler" guide. Do not invent rules that are not listed here. If the guide and the signed Preceptorship Agreement ever conflict, the Preceptorship Agreement controls.

---

## 1. Scope
- Applies to every NP student placed with a Preceptor Site preceptor.
- The policy is signed **once per rotation** (a new signature for every new rotation, even for returning students).
- **The online scheduler is the only official way to schedule clinical hours.** Text, phone, or in-person requests are not official.
- It is the **student's sole responsibility** to enter and submit their own hour requests.

## 2. Accessing the Scheduler
- Website: **sked.thepreceptorsite.com**
- Login: the **email address or Student ID** provided by The Preceptor Site.
- Login access is issued only **after the preceptor fee is paid** (see Section 4).
- Login trouble: email **coordinator@thepreceptorsite.com** with full name and university/program.

## 3. Session Statuses
| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | Student submitted a request. Nothing confirmed yet. Waiting on preceptor and/or coordinator review. |
| **Approved** | Preceptor and coordinator confirmed the date/time. Student is expected to attend. |
| **Denied** | Request not granted (preceptor unavailable, schedule full, policy issue). Student must request a different slot. |
| **Completed** | Session happened; hours logged and confirmed. Counts toward the rotation total. |
| **Cancelled** | An Approved session will not happen (cancelled by student, preceptor, or The Preceptor Site). No hours earned unless rescheduled and completed. |

- Only **Completed** hours count toward required rotation hours.
- **Response time:** Coordinator responds to Pending requests within **2 business days**. No reply after 2 business days = follow up. Silence is not a denial.
- **Makeup window:** missed hours may be made up up to **1 business day** after the rotation's official end date. After that, the rotation is closed.
- **Partial days:** a full day is expected. Finishing fewer than 8 hours (late arrival / early leave) needs **preceptor approval in advance**, or the partial day may not count.

## 4. Payment & Access
- No hour requests until the rotation's preceptor fee is **paid in full**.
- Scheduler access is granted only after payment is confirmed.
- Payment is non-refundable, per the Preceptorship Agreement.

## 5. Scheduling & Availability
- Slots are **first come, first served**. Submitting a request does not guarantee the slot — it still must be Approved.
- Availability depends on: preceptor availability, clinic holidays/closures, clinic business hours, and staffing.
- Request early for the best chance at preferred dates.
- Daily limit: **up to 8–8.5 hours per day**.
- Students **cannot schedule more total hours than they purchased** for the rotation.

Clinic hours: Mon–Thu 8:00 AM–5:00 PM (8-hour days); Fri 10:00 AM–3:00 PM (5-hour day).

## 6. Cancellations by the Student
- **Every cancellation requires written notice**, given either by email to coordinator@thepreceptorsite.com **or** through the scheduler (the **Request cancellation** button).
- **48+ hours notice:** send written notice at least 48 hours before the session.
- **Less than 48 hours:** send written notice **and** notify the preceptor directly, as soon as possible.
- The scheduler does not enforce these rules automatically; they are the student's responsibility.
- Repeated late cancellations may be reviewed as an integrity/professionalism concern.
- **No-show** (no notice at all): no hours credit; future dates may be denied or cancelled.
- **2 no-shows in one rotation = automatic notification to the student's university/program.**

## 7. Cancellations by The Preceptor Site or Preceptor
- Approved sessions may be cancelled for unforeseen reasons (illness, emergencies, closures, staffing).
- The Coordinator and/or Preceptor will notify the student ASAP and work to reschedule.
- These cancellations do **not** count against the student.

## 8. Auditing of Hours
- Hours may be audited at any time by: Preceptor Site staff, the preceptor, clinic staff, and the student's university/program staff.
- Audits may compare logged hours to sign-in records, clinic schedules, and preceptor notes. Students should keep their own records.
- **Disputes:** if logged hours look wrong, contact the Coordinator or preceptor as soon as possible.

## 9. Student Integrity Policy
1. **Accuracy** — logged hours must match actual time worked. Logging hours not worked is falsification.
2. **Honesty in requests** — requests must reflect true availability and intent to attend.
3. **Timeliness** — promptly report any conflict, delay, or absence (per Section 6).
4. **Professional conduct** — follow TMM Medical Group dress code, badge, and confidentiality rules (dress code lives in a separate policy / the Preceptorship Agreement).
5. **Consequences** — falsified hours, repeated no-shows, or other violations may lead to denied requests, termination of the rotation without refund, and/or notification of the university/program.

## 10. Governing Documents
- The Preceptorship Agreement controls if it conflicts with this policy.
