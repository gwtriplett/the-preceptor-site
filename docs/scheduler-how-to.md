# How To Use the Scheduler

Welcome! This guide walks you through the online scheduler at **sked.thepreceptorsite.com**: how to log in, request your clinical hours, read your session statuses, cancel, and check your progress.

A few things to know up front:

- **The scheduler is the only official way to schedule your clinical hours.** Requests made by text, phone, or in person aren't official.
- **Scheduling is your job.** You're responsible for entering and submitting your own hour requests.
- You'll sign the Clinical Hours Policy **once for each rotation**, even if you've rotated with us before.
- If this guide and your signed Preceptorship Agreement ever disagree, **the Preceptorship Agreement wins**.

---

## 1. Before you start

You'll get scheduler access **after your rotation's preceptor fee is paid in full**. Until payment is confirmed, you won't be able to request hours. (Payment is non-refundable, per the Preceptorship Agreement.)

---

## 2. Logging in

1. Go to **sked.thepreceptorsite.com**.
2. On the sign-in screen, click **Student**.
3. In the **Email address or Student ID** box, type the email address or Student ID The Preceptor Site gave you. Student IDs look like `26-2123`, and you'll find yours in your intake confirmation.
4. Click **Access My Sessions**.

**Can't get in?** Email **coordinator@thepreceptorsite.com** with your full name and your university/program.

---

## 3. Finding your way around

Once you're in, you'll see:

- **Your hours at the top:** hours completed, hours unscheduled (still to book), and a progress bar (more on this in section 7).
- **Your rotation cards:** one card per rotation, each with its own hours tracker.
- **Tabs:**
  - **My Sessions:** every session you've requested and its status. This is where you request and cancel sessions.
  - **Update Info:** ask for a change to your phone, address, or school supervisor.
  - **Message Coordinator:** send a message to your coordinator and see their replies.
  - **My Availability:** let us know about dates you're not available.
  - **Clinical Rotations:** your rotations and their details.
  - **Scheduling Policies:** the scheduling rules.

---

## 4. Requesting hours

1. Go to the **My Sessions** tab and click **+ Request a session**.
2. Pick **which rotation** the dates are for.
3. Enter the **Requested Date**, your **Arrival Time** and **Departure Time**, and the **Estimated Hours**.
4. Add your **Clinical Focus / What you hope to work on**, plus any **Notes for Preceptor** (optional).
5. Requesting several days? Click **+ Add another date for this student** and add each one, so you can submit them all together.
6. Click **Submit**.

Your info is filled in from your student profile. If something's wrong, use **Update Info** or message your coordinator.

### Good to know when you request

- **First come, first served.** Submitting a request doesn't lock in the slot. It still has to be **Approved**.
- **Request early** for the best chance at the dates you want.
- What's available depends on preceptor availability, clinic holidays and closures, clinic business hours, and staffing.
- **Daily limit:** up to **8–8.5 hours per day**.
- **You can't schedule more total hours than you purchased** for your rotation.
- **Plan for full days.** A full day is expected. If you'll finish fewer than 8 hours (arriving late or leaving early), get your **preceptor's approval in advance**, or the partial day may not count.

**Clinic hours:** Monday–Thursday 8:00 AM–5:00 PM (8-hour days) and Friday 10:00 AM–3:00 PM (5-hour day).

---

## 5. Reading your session statuses

Each session in **My Sessions** shows a status:

| Status | What it means | What you see in the portal |
|---|---|---|
| **Pending** | You sent the request. Nothing is confirmed yet. It's waiting on preceptor and/or coordinator review. | 🟡 *Awaiting approval* |
| **Approved** | Your preceptor and coordinator confirmed the date and time. You're expected to attend. | 🟢 *Cleared to show up* |
| **Denied** | The request wasn't granted (for example, the preceptor isn't available, the schedule is full, or there's a policy issue). Request a different slot. | 🔴 *Not approved — contact coordinator* |
| **Completed** | The session happened, and your hours were logged and confirmed. These hours count toward your rotation total. | Completed |
| **Cancelled** | An Approved session won't happen. You, your preceptor, or The Preceptor Site cancelled it. No hours are earned unless it's rescheduled and completed. | Cancelled |

### How long does approval take?

Your coordinator responds to **Pending** requests within **2 business days**. If you haven't heard back after 2 business days, follow up. **No reply doesn't mean no.** It isn't a denial.

---

## 6. Cancelling a session

Life happens. Here's how to cancel the right way.

**Every cancellation needs written notice.** You can give it either way:

- **In the scheduler:** go to **My Sessions**, find the Pending or Approved session, and click **Request cancellation**. It changes to *⏳ Cancellation requested — waiting for your coordinator.*
- **By email:** write to your coordinator at **coordinator@thepreceptorsite.com**.

**At least 48 hours before the session:** send your written notice at least 48 hours ahead.

**Less than 48 hours before the session:** send your written notice **and** tell your preceptor directly, as soon as you can.

**Don't assume the session is cancelled until your coordinator confirms.**

### Please keep in mind

- **Repeated late cancellations** may be reviewed as an integrity or professionalism concern.
- **No-show (no notice at all):** you get no hours credit, and future dates may be denied or cancelled.
- **Two no-shows in one rotation:** your university/program is automatically notified.

### If we cancel on you

Sometimes an Approved session has to be cancelled for unforeseen reasons, like illness, an emergency, a clinic closure, or staffing. Your coordinator and/or preceptor will let you know ASAP and work with you to reschedule. **These cancellations don't count against you.**

---

## 7. Checking your hours

At the top of your portal you'll see:

- **Hours completed**
- **Hours unscheduled**: hours you still need to book
- **A progress bar** showing how far you are toward your goal, plus how many hours are scheduled.

Each rotation card also has its own hours tracker.

**Only Completed hours count** toward your required rotation hours. Pending and Approved sessions are on the way, but they don't count until they're Completed.

### Making up missed hours

You can make up missed hours up to **1 business day after your rotation's official end date**. After that, the rotation is closed.

### Keep your own records

Your hours may be audited at any time by Preceptor Site staff, your preceptor, clinic staff, or your university/program. Audits may compare your logged hours to sign-in records, clinic schedules, and preceptor notes, so it's smart to keep your own records too.

**Hours look wrong?** Contact your coordinator or preceptor as soon as possible.

---

## 8. Integrity: the short version

- **Log only hours you actually worked.** Logging hours you didn't work is falsification.
- **Only request times you truly plan to attend.**
- **Report conflicts, delays, or absences promptly** (see section 6).
- **Follow TMM Medical Group's dress code, badge, and confidentiality rules.** These are in a separate policy and your Preceptorship Agreement.

Falsified hours, repeated no-shows, or other violations may lead to denied requests, ending the rotation without a refund, and/or notifying your university/program.
